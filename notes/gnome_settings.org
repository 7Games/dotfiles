#+TITLE: Gnome settings

I use Gnome as my desktop environment. Here are my settings to make it
actually usable.

* Themes

 * Adwaita light/dark theme for gtk3 (https://github.com/lassekongo83/adw-gtk3)

* Tweaks

** Keyboard

 * Swap Ctrl and Caps Lock - ON

** Windows

 * Maximise - ON
 * Minimise - ON
 * Placement - Right
 * Centre New Windows - ON
 * Resize with Secondary-Click - ON

* Settings

** Mouse & Touchpad

 * Corner Push - ON
 * Disable Touchpad While Typing - OFF
 * Tap to Click - OFF
 * Traditional Scroll Direction - ON

** Keyboard

*** Custom shortcuts

 * Launch Emacs - Super+E - *emacs*
 * Launch terminal - Super+Return - *gnome-terminal*

* Extensions

 * Appindicator and KStatusNotifierItem Support
 * Blur my Shell
 * Caffeine
 * GSConnect
 * Just Perfection
 * Logo Menu
